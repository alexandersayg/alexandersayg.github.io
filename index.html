Case Study

Freelance Project

Social Media Growth & Content Strategy

Inkiri Fitness

Canada · Remote · FreelanceJuly–October 2024

Project Overview

Inkiri Fitness wanted to grow its Instagram presence and attract a larger, more engaged audience within the fitness niche. My role involved identifying content formats that resonated with viewers, refining content direction and using performance insights to guide account growth.

The Challenge

When I joined, the Instagram account had approximately 700 followers and primarily featured general fitness videos.

The objective was to grow the account by identifying content that would capture attention, encourage viewers to stay and give them a reason to follow.

The Approach

During the first month, I published different types of general fitness content to understand audience behaviour and identify what worked.

I observed that viewers showed stronger interest in educational fitness videos addressing specific questions and problems, such as reducing belly fat and developing arm muscles.

Based on these observations, I refined the content direction and focused more on formats that consistently performed well.

Execution

  • Analysed content performance, including views, watch time, saves and shares.
  • Observed audience preferences to identify high-performing content formats.
  • Used the first month to assess different content approaches and establish performance patterns.
  • Doubled down on educational fitness content in the second month based on formats that performed consistently.
  • Continued refining content selection and maintaining consistency through the third and fourth months.
  • Used performance insights to guide subsequent content decisions.

Results

700 → 23,000
Followers in four months
4.1M+
Views on the highest-performing individual video
6
Videos crossed one million views

Key Takeaway

Growth came from understanding what the audience wanted to watch, identifying patterns in content performance and consistently adapting the strategy around what worked.
